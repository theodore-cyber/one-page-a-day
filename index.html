One Page a Day

Build a Reading Habit That Lasts

The barrier to reading isn't time — it's starting. One Page a Day removes that barrier entirely.


Commit to reading just one page every day. That's it. One page is so minimal you can't say no, but once you start, you usually keep going.

What Makes It Different

Western Canon Tracking: Mark off the great books you've read and track your progress through foundational literature.
Habit Formation, Not Achievement: No leaderboards, no social comparison. Just you, your books, and a simple daily question: "Did you read today?"
Classic Library Aesthetic: Warm browns, serif fonts, vintage textures. It feels like a library, not a social media app.
Meaningful Analytics: Track your streaks, pages read over time, books completed, and reading patterns.
Privacy First: All your data stays on your device. No accounts, no tracking, no data collection.
